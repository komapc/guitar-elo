How it works

Answer with sliders, and skip the silly questions. Each answer becomes practice hours, and the hours become a chess rating on one shared curve. A guitarist rated 1500 has put about as many hours into the guitar as a chess player rated 1500 has put into chess.

  • How it is scored: the curve, its calibration, and what chess players’ hours say about the rating.
  • Scale and comparisons: the rating bands, music school, conservatory and professional hours, and Vysotsky.
